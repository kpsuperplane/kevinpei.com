**4174**\. That’s the number of weeks in an 80-year lifespan. When I first read about this on Tim Urban’s [“Wait But Why?”](https://waitbutwhy.com/2014/05/life-weeks.html) I was just 17 years old and, at the time, it felt like a constant reminder to make the absolute most of every second I had. I even put it on my new tab page. 

<LifeWeeksWidget />

In the 10 years since, I’ve organized [Canada’s biggest hackathon](https://hackthenorth.medium.com/almost-every-time-our-servers-crashed-d6b53dba2b0), helped bring next-generation [display glasses](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7gtc1DW2Tgo) to life, and built a little [smart-home plugin](https://github.com/kpsuperplane/homebridge-wiz-lan) that somehow ended up on [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2021/09/how-i-saved-money-on-homekit-smartbulbs-with-philips-wiz-and-a-raspberry-pi/). I’ve also attended countless music festivals, discovered my love for [exploring](https://www.instagram.com/p/DVEnfk1EgfT/) the mountains, and become the proud owner of six pairs of skis.

But somewhere in there I also burned out. I got lost in the corporate grind, felt increasingly [ disillusioned](https://layoffs.fyi/) with the tech industry, and wondered one too many times, “what am I doing with my life?”

So here I am. The weeks are still counting down, but maximizing every second of my life isn’t sustainable. Hot chocolate after skiing is important too, and what matters is that I choose how I spend my time intentionally.

This space is me getting back in touch with my passion, creating for the sake of creating, and sharing whatever I can’t stop thinking about. 

Welcome to my little corner of the internet. Don’t mind the construction; I’m still renovating.  
I’m glad you’re here.
