Every blog starts with a hello world. This one is no different, except the player controls are fake and the scrubber tracks how far you've scrolled.

## Why a music player?

I've been thinking about how much of the web is just... expected. Articles look like articles. Portfolios look like portfolios. You know within half a second what kind of site you've landed on, and you've already decided how much you care.

The music player interface is the opposite of that. It's familiar — everyone has used Spotify or Apple Music — but seeing it on a personal website creates a moment of genuine disorientation. *Wait, what is this?* That pause is the whole point.

## What this site is

This is where I write about things I find interesting: software, systems, ideas, occasional tangents. The "tracks" are posts. The "playlists" are categories. The scrubber at the bottom tracks your reading progress, and yes, you can drag it to jump around.

No algorithms. No recommendations. Just a weird music player that contains thoughts.

Welcome.
